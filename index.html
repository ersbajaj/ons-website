What we do

Four disciplines, one point of accountability

Each service stands on its own, or runs together as a single managed package across your order — from the first datasheet to installation on site.

Engineering for rotating & packaged equipment

We support the technical decisions behind every purchase order, from equipment selection through to vendor sign-off.

  • Selection of the correct type of equipment for the application
  • Preparation of mechanical datasheets
  • Development of material requisitions
  • Technical bid evaluation
  • Vendor document review
  • Purchase order follow-up through regular technical meetings with the vendor

Expediting

We keep manufacturing and delivery schedules on track through direct, continuous contact with the vendor.

  • Desk expediting, tracking progress and documentation from our offices
  • Field expediting at the manufacturing location, with engineers on the shop floor

Inspection

We verify equipment quality and conformity at every stage of manufacture, against the codes that matter.

  • Inspection of rotating and packaged equipment in accordance with applicable codes and standards, including API
  • Pre-inspection meetings
  • Factory acceptance tests
  • In-stage inspections throughout production

Package management

We take ownership of the full order lifecycle, so nothing is lost at the handoffs between engineering, procurement and site.

  • Complete package management from purchase order placement through to delivery
  • Coordination through to final installation on site

How we work

How an order moves through ONS

A typical engagement runs across four stages, though we're glad to join at any point in the process.

01

Specification & bid evaluation

Equipment selection, mechanical datasheets, material requisitions and technical bid evaluation set the baseline before a purchase order is placed.

02

Vendor follow-up & expediting

Vendor document review and regular technical meetings track the purchase order, while desk and field expediting keep manufacturing on schedule.

03

Inspection & testing

Pre-inspection meetings, in-stage inspection and factory acceptance tests confirm the equipment meets API and other applicable codes.

04

Delivery & installation

Package management carries the order through shipment, delivery and installation, closing the loop from PO to plant.

Why teams bring us in

Sourcing rotating and packaged equipment touches engineering, procurement and quality all at once. Splitting that work across separate parties creates gaps at the handoffs — a vendor delay that goes unnoticed, or a datasheet detail that never reaches the inspector.

ONS keeps one team across the full order, so decisions made at the datasheet stage are still visible when equipment reaches factory acceptance testing.

  • 01Technical bid evaluation and vendor document review handled by the same engineers who wrote the datasheet
  • 02Field expediting at the manufacturing location, not just status calls from a desk
  • 03Inspection against API and other applicable codes and standards, with pre-inspection meetings built in
  • 04Package management that carries responsibility through to delivery and installation
